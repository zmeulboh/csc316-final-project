The 10-year Treasury yield is going crazy. Why is Wall Street so worried about a number you’ve barely heard of, and what could it mean for you?

Leo is born in Toronto in 2007. You'll watch him grow up one year at a time while the bar at the top tracks the number Wall Street is worried about: the US 10-year Treasury yield.

The rates up to October 2026 are real. Leo, his family and their numbers are fictional examples. Use Next, the → key or Space to move through the story.
