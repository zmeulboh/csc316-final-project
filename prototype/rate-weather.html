Act 3 · From Wall Street to Toronto

The domino chain

Each domino is a real dataset, 2019 to today. A higher safe rate on US bonds pushes up Canadian bond yields, which push up what banks charge for mortgages, which squeezes household budgets and spending, which reaches the job market you're about to enter.

Chains like this play out over months to years, and other forces push on every link.
